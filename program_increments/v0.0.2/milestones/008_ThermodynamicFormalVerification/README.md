## 1. Executive Summary
Milestone 008 extends Lean 4 formal verification across thermodynamic potentials and stoichiometric hypergraphs. It formally proves that irreversible STC transformations satisfy the Second Law of Thermodynamics (non-decreasing universal entropy $\Delta S_{\text{univ}} \ge 0$) and that atomic stoichiometry is conserved across reaction cascades.
